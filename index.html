Who We Are

Finance Support That Stays Close to the Business

SV CFO Plus is a unit of Shree Vasuda Venture Private Limited. We support businesses with accounting, finance, MIS, virtual CFO and business support services.

Our objective is simple: bring discipline to financial information and make it easier for management to understand performance, plan ahead and take timely decisions.

We combine accounting discipline, financial understanding and business perspective so that management receives information that is useful for action — not simply for records. We work as an extension of your team while keeping responsibility, review and communication clearly defined.
