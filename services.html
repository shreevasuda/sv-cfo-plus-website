What We Do

Our Services

A full range of services from bookkeeping and reporting through to virtual CFO and credit support — engage a single service or a continuing support arrangement.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Clean books, closed on a calendar you can plan around. We pick up the ledger wherever it stands today — current, behind, or in need of a rebuild.

  • Day-to-day transaction processing and coding
  • Bank, credit card and merchant reconciliations
  • Accounts payable and accounts receivable ledgers
  • Month-end and year-end close with checklists
  • Multi-entity and multi-currency accounting
  • Fixed asset register and depreciation schedules
  • Inventory and cost of goods accounting
  • Catch-up, clean-up and prior-period rectification
  • Ledger schedules and financial statement support
  • Chart of accounts design and restructuring

MIS & Management Reporting

The monthly pack that actually gets read — numbers with the commentary that explains what moved and why it matters.

  • Monthly MIS and management reporting packs
  • Dashboards and KPI reporting
  • Budgeting, forecasting and rolling re-forecasts
  • Budget-to-actual variance analysis with commentary
  • Cash flow statements and short-term cash forecasting
  • Product, branch and customer profitability analysis
  • Costing, margin and break-even analysis
  • Working capital and ageing analysis

Virtual CFO

Senior finance thinking without a senior finance salary — for the questions a bookkeeper cannot answer and a full-time CFO is too early to hire for.

  • Periodic financial review with management
  • Financial planning and budgeting support
  • Cash-flow visibility and runway analysis
  • Board, investor and lender reporting packs
  • Pricing, unit economics and contribution analysis
  • Working capital and funding structure review
  • Finance function design and team structuring
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis

Financial Advisory

Structured analysis for the decisions that carry a number — investment, expansion, funding and restructuring.

  • Financial modelling and three-statement projections
  • Detailed project reports and business plans
  • Feasibility studies and viability analysis
  • Ratio analysis — DSCR, break-even and return measures
  • Funding option comparison and cost-of-funds analysis
  • Investor and lender presentations
  • Cost optimisation and financial structuring
  • Management decision support and analysis

Business Support

The repetitive, deadline-bound work that fills your team’s day and none of their job satisfaction.

  • Invoice processing and vendor master management
  • Purchase order and three-way matching
  • Customer invoicing, statements and collection follow-up
  • Payroll input preparation and coordination
  • Employee expense and reimbursement processing
  • Document indexing, digitisation and records management
  • Standard operating procedure documentation
  • Process review and management coordination

Credit & Project Support

Lender-ready papers, built the way credit teams actually read them — drawn from years of banking and project finance work.

  • CMA data preparation
  • Working capital and term loan proposals
  • Project finance documentation
  • Credit facility structuring, fund-based and non-fund-based
  • Import and export limit documentation
  • Loan syndication support
  • Assistance on sanction terms and conditions
  • Sanction condition tracking and documentation

Compliance & Coordination Support

Reviewer-ready files, prepared to the standard your own licensed professional expects to receive.

  • Working paper and schedule preparation
  • Trial balance to return reconciliation
  • Data preparation for statutory filings
  • Reconciliation of tax data to books
  • Audit support schedules and auditor query responses
  • Compliance calendars and filing status tracking
  • Coordination with your auditors and tax advisers
  • Internal control documentation support
Preparation and analytical support only. Filing, certification, attestation and professional sign-off remain with your own licensed Chartered Accountant, CPA, ACCA or tax agent.

We price on the shape of the work, not on a rate card published before we have seen your books. After an introductory call and a look at your volumes, you receive a written fee proposal. There is no charge for the introductory call or the scoping exercise, and a short paid pilot is available if you would like to see the work before committing to a term.

Confidentiality

Data Security & Confidentiality

Your books and your client data are the most sensitive things you will hand to a support partner. We treat them that way.

Contractual Protection

  • Non-disclosure agreement signed before any data is shared
  • Individual confidentiality undertaking from every assigned team member
  • Defined data ownership, retention and return-or-destroy terms on exit

Access Controls

  • Work performed within your systems and access arrangements wherever possible
  • Role-based access reviewed whenever the engagement team changes
  • Multi-factor authentication and no shared credentials

Working Environment

  • Controlled workstations and a clean-desk policy
  • Encrypted, access-controlled file transfer — never personal email
  • Processes designed around India's DPDP Act, 2023 and client-side privacy requirements
